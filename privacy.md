# Perksly Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 30, 2026

## Summary

Perksly does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. Everything you enter — your cards, benefits, perks, and usage history — stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Perksly has no servers that receive your data. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

- Your name, email address, or contact information
- Your credit card numbers, account balances, or financial details
- The cards you add, the benefits you log, or any usage history
- Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data
- Crash reports or analytics events

## Where your data lives

All information you enter into Perksly is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. It is included in your standard device backups (iCloud Backup or encrypted iTunes/Finder backup) only if you have those features enabled — those backups are managed by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not by us.

To delete all your Perksly data, simply delete the app from your device.

## Catalog updates

Perksly periodically checks a public GitHub repository ([perksly-data](https://github.com/WTL7/perksly-data)) for updates to the built-in card catalog (e.g., when a new card is added or a benefit changes). This is a one-way download of public data — your device fetches the catalog file. We do not send any information about you, your device, or your cards in this request beyond what your network connection routinely exposes (such as your IP address) to GitHub, who hosts the file under [GitHub's privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Third-party services

Perksly does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

## Children's privacy

Perksly is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and note the change in the app's release notes.
